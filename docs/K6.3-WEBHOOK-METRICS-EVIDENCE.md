# K6.3 slice: content-free webhook delivery metrics

Issue #376 (parent #48, checklist item K6.3). PR #377 merged as
`3d247f4665ce4d6a75358c7ffac8bdeffe738109`; contract CI #615 passed on reviewed head
`0b1218c2acecc9a723f1c7b872a9dab70103bd32`. This slice adds content-free Prometheus series for
webhook fanout backlog, tenant discovery, leases, attempts and outcomes through the
`webhook-worker` management endpoint.

## Discovery metadata

Migration `0050_webhook_delivery_metrics` replaces
`keel_meta.list_webhook_delivery_tenants(limit)` with the same `keel_webhook` gate, ordering and
1-1000 bound, plus:

- `r_due_at`, the earliest due or expired-lease time for the tenant;
- `r_due_count`, the number of due or expired deliveries for the tenant.

It still returns only tenant ID, due time and count. It exposes no delivery, endpoint, URL,
payload, attempt or state data.

## Series

`internal/delivery/webhook/metrics` records:

- `keel_webhook_discovery_passes_total`
- `keel_webhook_discovery_errors_total`
- `keel_webhook_due_tenants`
- `keel_webhook_due_deliveries`
- `keel_webhook_oldest_due_age_seconds`
- `keel_webhook_last_discovery_timestamp_seconds`
- `keel_webhook_tenant_errors_total`
- `keel_webhook_attempts_started_total`
- `keel_webhook_outcomes_total{outcome=...}` with fixed outcomes `delivered`, `retry_scheduled`,
  `exhausted`, `failed`, `deferred` and `skipped`

No tenant, endpoint, event, delivery, URL, payload or error string is a label. The values are
process-local and reset on restart. Due gauges retain the last successful discovery values when a
later discovery fails; the failure increments `discovery_errors_total`. A successful pass updates
the last-discovery timestamp.

The worker observes the due backlog created by fanout, not a producer-side fanout counter.
`due_deliveries` and `oldest_due_age_seconds` are the worker-visible fanout signal. There is no
dashboard, alert rule, SLO qualification or durable reconciliation in this slice.

## Wiring

`postgres.TenantSource` validates the tenant ID, nonzero due time and positive due count, and
combines defensive duplicate rows. `scheduler` defines a content-free observer interface; `RunOnce`
reports one pass observation and one tenant observation per batch. `webhook-worker` passes a
`webhookmetrics.Recorder` to the scheduler and to `health.RunWithMetrics`, so `/metrics` includes
the webhook series alongside the standard process and HTTP metrics.

## Verification

Coverage runs against a fresh PostgreSQL database with migrations through `0050`:

- discovery returns the earliest due time and due count, including grouping of multiple due
  deliveries for one tenant;
- due/expired tenants are included, future pending and future in-flight tenants are excluded, and
  the limit is honored;
- the application role cannot execute discovery and the webhook role cannot read state directly;
- the recorder renders the expected fixed series, retains backlog after a discovery failure and is
  safe under concurrent observation;
- scheduler observations carry the due counts and per-tenant failure flag without identifiers;
- runtime tests and `webhook-worker` registration continue to pass.

Linux CI covers the race detector.
