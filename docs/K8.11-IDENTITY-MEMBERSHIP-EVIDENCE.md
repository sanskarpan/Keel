# K8.11/K8.2 slice 17: membership lifecycle over HTTP

Related to #70 and the K8.1/K8.2 tracks. Storage proved role changes,
removal and reap; this slice serves them on four unmounted routes with
the session principal as the only actor. No route is mounted.

## Contract

- Migration `0079_identity_membership_lifecycle`: replaces the
  memberships guard to allow exactly the lifecycle transitions —
  owner/admin/member↔admin/member role moves except into or out of the
  owner seat, and deletes of non-owner rows (owners leave only by
  transfer) — plus three capability-gated functions:
  `set_organization_member_role` (owner-only; `not_authorized`,
  `not_member`, `owner_immutable`, idempotent `unchanged`),
  `remove_organization_member` (owner/admin remove others, non-owners
  leave themselves, owners never leave), and
  `reap_expired_organization_invites` (negative on unauthorized,
  otherwise the deleted count).
- `internal/auth/httpapi/directory.go` additions:
  `POST .../members/{id}/role` (`200` with outcome, `403`/`404`/
  `409 owner_immutable`, owner-role and unknown-role `400`),
  `POST .../members/{id}/remove` (`204`, `403`/`404`/`409`),
  `POST .../invites/reap` (`200 {reaped}`).
- `internal/auth/postgres`: `SetMemberRole` / `RemoveMember` /
  `ReapExpiredInvites` with sentinel mappings.

## Verified

- `go vet ./internal/auth/...` and `gofmt` clean;
  `go test ./internal/auth/...` pass, including mapping groups for
  all three routes.
- Migration applied live through the runner (`0079` ledgered).
- In-container against real capability roles:
  `--- PASS: TestPostgreSQLIdentityMembershipLifecycle` — promote,
  idempotent re-promote, demote, non-owner and owner-seat refusals,
  leave, remove, owner-leave and remove-owner refusals, reap with
  stranger refusal and repeat zero;
  `--- PASS: TestHTTPMembershipLifecycleAgainstPostgres` — invite,
  accept, promote, non-owner promotion refusal, demote, leave with
  empty post-leave listing, stranger reap refusal, reap zero.
- All earlier suites pass in the same runs.

## Limits

- No transfer-via-HTTP changes, no member directory pagination, no
  audit trail.
- The owner seat still moves only by transfer; no demotion/removal
  escape hatch exists by design.
