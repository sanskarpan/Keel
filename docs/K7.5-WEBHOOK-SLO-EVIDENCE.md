# K7.5 slice: webhook delivery SLOs and burn alerts

Related to #56 (K7.5 partial; does not close it). This slice defines owner-decided webhook
SLOs with multiwindow burn-rate alerts. No Go changes.

## Contract

- SLO-1: 99% of started attempts end `delivered` over 30 days; error outcomes are
  `failed`/`exhausted` only (`retry_scheduled`, `deferred`, `skipped` are flow control).
  Fast burn (>2% over 1h and 5m, critical) and slow burn (>1% over 6h and 30m, warning).
  Zero attempts yields NaN, which never fires.
- SLO-2 (freshness target): 99.5% of time with oldest due age under one hour, signaled by
  the existing oldest-due alert until a gauge-SLO recording exists.
- `deploy/monitoring/prometheus/keel-webhook-slo-test.yaml` proves the burn math both
  directions with synthetic series (3% fires both alerts, 0% stays silent).
- SRE table, dashboard contract panels and gate audit updated.

## Limits

The local 2-hour TSDB cannot evaluate day-scale windows: rules are syntax- and
math-validated, meaningful on a production Prometheus. No paging keys, escalation policy
or production deployment.
