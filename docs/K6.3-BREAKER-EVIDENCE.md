# K6.3 slice: per-endpoint circuit breaker policy

Issue #344 (parent #48, checklist item K6.3). This slice adds a pure, per-endpoint circuit breaker
policy that complements the retry scheduler: retry decides when the next attempt is due, the breaker
decides whether an attempt may start at all. The policy is not persisted or wired into a worker,
because endpoint registration does not exist yet.

## Policy

| Bound | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `FailureThreshold` | 5 | Consecutive `retryable` outcomes that open a closed breaker |
| `OpenFor` | 30s | First cooldown while open |
| `MaxOpenFor` | 10m | Ceiling for an escalated cooldown |
| `ProbesPerHalfOpen` | 1 | Attempts admitted concurrently after the cooldown |

`Validate` rejects a threshold outside 1-100, a base cooldown outside 1s-1h, a maximum below the
base or above 24h, and a probe budget outside 1-10.

## States and transitions

`BreakerState` is a small value type with a `BreakerStatus`, consecutive failures, the time the
breaker opened, the cooldown in force and the probes already admitted. Its zero value is a closed
breaker with no failures, so a new endpoint needs no initialization.

| From | Event | To | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| closed | attempt allowed | closed | None; the caller may start one attempt |
| closed | `retryable` | closed | Increment consecutive failures |
| closed | `retryable` at the threshold | open | Cooldown starts at `OpenFor` |
| closed | `delivered` or `permanent` | closed | Clear failures |
| open | attempt allowed before `OpenedAt+OpenFor` | open | Denied |
| open | attempt allowed at or after the cooldown | half-open | Admit one probe; probe counter starts |
| open | any outcome | open | Ignored |
| half-open | attempt beyond the probe budget | half-open | Denied |
| half-open | `delivered` or `permanent` | closed | Clear failures, cooldown and probes |
| half-open | `retryable` | open | Double the cooldown, capped at `MaxOpenFor` |

`Allow(now, state)` returns the possibly transitioned state and whether one attempt may start. The
caller must persist the returned state even when the decision is deny, because an elapsed cooldown
moves the breaker to half-open. `Observe(now, state, outcome)` records one completed attempt.
`OpenUntil()` reports the cooldown end only while open.

## Decisions worth stating

- Only `retryable` outcomes count as failures. A `delivered` or `permanent` response proves the
  endpoint is reachable even when the specific request was rejected, so both reset the breaker.
  This avoids keeping an endpoint open because its payloads are permanently invalid.
- Outcomes that arrive while the breaker is open are ignored. A slow attempt that finishes after
  the endpoint already tripped cannot extend or clear the cooldown, which keeps the policy stable
  under concurrent workers.
- A failed half-open probe doubles the cooldown, so a flapping endpoint backs off instead of
  probing at a fixed rate. A success resets the escalation, so the next trip starts at `OpenFor`
  again rather than at the previous maximum.
- The clock is supplied by the caller, matching the retry scheduler. The policy never sleeps,
  records metrics or persists anything.
- `Observe` rejects an unknown outcome and both methods reject a state that violates its own
  invariants (for example an open state without an open time or cooldown).

## Coverage

Unit tests cover policy bounds, the zero state, threshold counting, denial during the cooldown,
admission exactly at the cooldown boundary, single and multi-probe half-open behavior, failed-probe
reopening with a doubled cooldown, escalation through repeated failures and the cap, reset by
`delivered` and `permanent`, ignored outcomes while open, `OpenUntil`, invalid outcomes, invalid
policies and invalid states.

## Boundaries and open work

There is no endpoint registry, so the policy is keyed by nothing and persisted nowhere; a caller
would hold one `BreakerState` per endpoint. There is no worker to consult `Allow` before a lease,
no decision yet on how a denied attempt is rescheduled (the delivery should remain `pending` with a
bounded delay rather than consume an attempt), no durable breaker state across restarts, no metric
for open/half-open endpoints, no operator reset path and no integration with the attempt log.
`K6.3` and parent #48 remain open. See `docs/K6.3-RETRY-EVIDENCE.md` for the scheduling half.
