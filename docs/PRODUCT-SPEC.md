# Keel product behavior beyond the technical baseline

Normative expansion specification, revision 2. SPEC.md continues to govern core event, retrieval, AI and failure contracts; this file adds the SaaS/purchasing behavior in the versioned catalog. The shared SaaS foundation is normative for organization/identity/billing. Features have minimum release versions; no future version is implied to be implemented today.

## 1. Intake, master data and supplier collaboration — 1.0

Purchase category definitions contain a schema/version, conditional fields, permitted supplier classes, evidence requirements, budget mapping and approval route reference. Only a bounded declarative form/rule language is accepted; no tenant-supplied code. Published definitions are immutable versions. Drafts reference their original version; submission migrates explicitly or validates under a still-allowed version. Schema changes cannot silently reinterpret monetary/currency/identity fields.

A submitted request references one tenant-owned supplier, line quantities/prices, currency, business scope, desired date, policy and evidence versions. Human confirmation of AI-extracted fields is auditable; generated prose is not evidence of authority. The order remains the request's event-sourced approval aggregate in 1.0. Introduce separate fulfillment aggregates in 1.5 without changing historical `order.approved` meaning.

Supplier master stores legal/trading names, verified contact references, external identifiers and status `draft/reviewing/eligible/suspended/archived`. Onboarding cases are assessment episodes, not supplier identity. Duplicate candidates use same-tenant normalized identifiers and scored similarity. A coordinator reviews a merge; canonical supplier ID persists, aliases redirect, historical evidence/orders retain original references and audit. No cross-tenant matching or automatic merge from AI similarity.

Supplier portal membership binds supplier principal, tenant and supplier ID plus explicit case/PO grants. A global principal can have independent grants from multiple buying organizations; that is not a shared supplier-data network. External views expose only allowed tasks/documents/threads. Supplier contact updates affecting trusted identifiers need buyer review. Invitation acceptance never grants an internal user role. Download/upload grants bind object/version/purpose and reauthorize independently of knowing the record UUID.

## 2. Approval plans, delegation and deadlines — 1.0; policy simulation — 2.0

Material submission freezes an approval packet: resource/version, evidence digest, policy version, supplier eligibility revision, budget amount/reference, form version and required steps. 1.0 supports serial or parallel steps with explicit eligible role/team and all-required-step semantics. A quorum feature needs its own policy schema/qualification; it is not inferred from an array of approvers. Default deny on unresolved role or no eligible approver; show coordinator remediation.

`approval_plans` and `approval_steps` record dependencies, eligible scopes, deadlines, status and supersession. Every accepted decision locks the authoritative case/order and active plan, rechecks current identity/role/delegation, packet digest and DB time, then appends a decision/step event. For an order, only the transaction completing all required approvals appends `order.approved` and converts its procurement reservation to a commitment. Earlier step decisions append explicit progress events and cannot authorize PO issuance. A configured rejecting step ends the plan/resource; partial approvals cannot override it.

For onboarding, step decisions and signal intents are durable; Temporal reads the plan outcome through activities. Deadline arbitration under the resource lock accepts only a completed eligible plan with the required decisions accepted before their effective deadlines. An early first approval does not defeat expiry while other required approvals are absent. Expiry and final decision serialize; delayed signals cannot lose a valid completed plan. Default case deadline remains 72 hours; per-step deadlines cannot silently extend it.

Delegation binds delegator/delegate, role/amount/currency/business scope, start/end interval and reason, accepted by an authorized administrator or constrained delegation policy. Delegate must already possess the necessary base permission, cannot approve a self-request, and cannot recursively delegate or broaden limits. Revoking delegation blocks new decisions. Historical accepted decisions remain unless an explicit authorized invalidation event is applied under the resource lock.

Material request/evidence changes supersede the plan and require fresh approvals/reservation adjustment. Nonmaterial comment/description changes are classified by published policy; they never rewrite the approved packet. Policy publication affects new packets; applying it to pending packets requires explicit migration/supersession. In 2.0 simulation evaluates historical read-only scenarios against a proposed version, showing missing reviewers, introduced reviews, scope changes and completeness. No live decisions, model writes or notifications. Bound to 10,000 requests/job, two jobs/tenant and fixed time/resource profile initially; publication requires validated acyclic routes and authorized review.

## 3. Procurement commitment ledger — 1.0

Separate `procurement_budget_accounts` from AI `budget_accounts` and SaaS subscriptions. Accounts have tenant/entity/project/period/currency, limit, pending reserved amount, open committed amount, recognized accounting actuals and version. Baseline accounts are single-currency. Every request/order line, active approval threshold and budget account involved in a v1 decision must use the same ISO currency and its ISO-defined minor-unit exponent. Reject mixed-currency orders and threshold/account currency mismatches before submission, approval, or reservation with a stable validation error; never infer a conversion or compare unlike units. FX conversion requires the versioned, pinned quote flow in 2.0. Every request/order line, active approval threshold and budget account involved in a v1 decision must use the same ISO currency and its ISO-defined minor-unit exponent. Reject mixed-currency orders and threshold/account currency mismatches before submission, approval, or reservation with a stable validation error; never infer a conversion or compare unlike units. FX conversion requires the versioned, pinned quote flow in 2.0. Available = limit − pending reservations − open commitments − actuals, with adjustments/reversals explicit. An accounting actual replacing a covered commitment must atomically reduce that commitment; it cannot count both.

Submission locks affected accounts in deterministic order and creates a unique request reservation. Final approval atomically converts it to an open commitment; rejection/cancel releases it. Amendment reserves a positive delta before approval; a decrease releases only the approved delta, preserving issued obligation history. Reservations cannot silently expire while a valid pending request is using them: either renew under policy or record a superseded/expired request and force revalidation. Hard limits reject competing claims; overrides require a separate role, reason, bounded amount and audit, never AI tool authority.

Immutable `procurement_ledger_entries` carry logical operation/source, kind (`reserve`, `release`, `commit`, `actual`, `reverse`, `adjust`), currency/minor units and evidence. Derived counters/analytics reconcile against ledger sums. Uniqueness prevents duplicate ERP callbacks from reducing commitments twice. Reconciling unknown/inconsistent mapping freezes affected financial transitions and exposes an exception; it never assumes zero actuals. Opening balances/imports require provenance and operator review.

Enterprise multi-currency uses a versioned FX source, timestamp/rate, target budget currency, rounding rule and stale-rate policy. Quote is frozen per packet/commitment; execution or settlement FX differences become separately reviewed adjustments. Never sum INR and USD amounts directly or use binary float; report native currencies and converted views distinctly.

## 4. Collaboration, notifications and bulk data — 1.0

Resource conversations have internal or explicitly external visibility, participant allowlists and auditable edit history. Mention recipients must currently access the thread/resource; outbound notifications reauthorize at dispatch. A thread made external creates a new shared view after review, not a bulk flip revealing historical internal comments. Supplier uploads enter scan/publication paths rather than unchecked comment attachments.

Notification intents are transactional and deduplicated; quiet hours/digests apply except required security/legal notices. Approval remains in the authenticated console by default. Import dry-run validates scope, external keys, data types and routing. Commit batches up to 500 rows with per-row durable result, global job idempotency and bounded retries. Bad rows do not partially create hidden unreferenced financial records. Export caps and manifests follow the foundation; comments/supplier records use current visibility.

## 5. Catalog and sourcing — 1.5

Catalog items reference eligible supplier, approved price/currency/unit/terms, contract version, validity period and entity/project permissions. Repeat-buy templates copy allowed draft fields and revalidate current price/supplier/budget/approvals; they cannot clone a previous authorization. Catalog updates publish a new version and invalidate stale quotations when material.

Sourcing aggregate: `draft -> open -> closed -> awarded | canceled`. RFQ/RFP defines buyer criteria/version, invited suppliers, sealed visibility policy, deadline and permitted change process. Supplier responses are immutable submitted versions with allowed predeadline replacements. No supplier sees another bid. Deadline uses DB time; late responses are rejected or explicitly admitted with a visible exception/new evaluation round. AI normalizes/compares evidence but human award is a signed command with recorded criteria/rationale. Award creates draft request or supplier review; it does not bypass approval.

## 6. Issued POs, amendments and receipts — 1.5

PO aggregate separates purchasing authorization from fulfillment. States: `draft -> issued -> acknowledged -> partially_received -> received -> closed`, with rejected/canceled/change-pending branches. Issuance requires eligible approved request authority, current supplier eligibility or explicitly reviewed exception, matching line/value/currency and no other live PO consuming that authority beyond the approved allocation. Stable tenant/entity PO numbers and external IDs have permanent deduplication keys. Dispatch intent commits with issuance; delivery is asynchronous/idempotent.

Material amendment creates a proposed PO version, diff and linked fresh approval/budget delta. Issued original remains authoritative until amendment is approved and dispatched; late supplier acknowledgment binds the exact issued version. Unknown external dispatch results reconcile by stable PO/version before resending. Supplier proposed changes are not accepted by merely uploading a document.

The amendment opens a new linked order-approval aggregate (`request_kind=po_amendment`) naming the authoritative PO/base version and proposed full terms/total. Original approved order history remains terminal. Routing/amount thresholds evaluate the proposed full total and material change, while procurement reservation covers only the independently computed positive commitment delta; clients cannot choose a favorable reservation mode or approve only the delta to evade thresholds. Final approval of the linked aggregate authorizes one exact amendment. Applying it rechecks base version and converts/releases the delta once; a concurrent amendment supersedes/revalidates rather than sharing stale approval. A negative delta is released only when the amendment actually becomes authoritative, not when proposed.

Receipt records reference PO/version/line, accepted quantities or service milestone amounts, actor, date and evidence. Initially quantity uses fixed-point decimal with declared scale/unit; all money uses minor units/explicit rounding. Lock line counters and reject quantities beyond allowed tolerance or require audited receiving override. Partial receipt/return/reversal append records; do not edit/delete accepted receipts. PO cancellation of a partly received obligation requires a reviewed remainder action and corresponding commitment adjustment, not full release. Inventory stocks/warehouse allocation stay outside scope.

## 7. Invoice review and ERP handoff — 1.5

Invoice aggregate: `captured -> validated -> matching -> matched | exception -> reviewed -> exported -> reconciled`, with void/correction paths. Supplier+entity+normalized invoice number+currency and document digest identify duplicate candidates; ambiguous matches require review rather than permanent auto-collapse. Extracted invoice fields require confirmation/qualified policy before financial handoff.

Two-way matching compares invoice to issued PO; three-way also compares accepted receipts/milestones. Published tolerances cover unit price, quantity, currency, taxes/freight metadata and total differences. Line allocations cannot invoice accepted capacity twice. A qualified reviewer resolves each discrepancy with reason/evidence; no “paid” transition arises from local matching. Accounting adapter exports a stable invoice/PO/version ID, observes remote receipt and reconciles actuals. Payment/status observations are labeled as remote observations, with freshness/source; Keel never submits bank/card payment instructions.

Connector registry declares source of truth for supplier, account codes, budgets/actuals, PO and invoices. Per-record external mapping includes provider ID/version and local source version; conflicts go to the workbench. External calls stay outside DB locks, inbound callbacks are authenticated/deduplicated, and effect IDs survive retry/restore. Partial export can succeed remotely and lose response: observe before retry. Provider accounting locks and close periods are respected; corrections are linked adjustments, never history rewrites.

## 8. Contracts, risk and renewals — 1.5/2.0

Contract metadata references immutable documents, supplier, entity, owner, effective/end dates, notice period, value/currency and obligations. Human confirmation records source spans and certainty. Date-only notice deadlines use the legal entity's declared timezone/calendar; reminder rules are durable scheduled jobs/workflows. Renewal opens a new approval request with eligibility/current evidence/budget checks. Unconfirmed dates or missing owner create exceptions, not guessed reminders. Native e-signature is deferred to a qualified provider integration in 3.0.

Enterprise risk assessments reference rubric/version, questionnaire responses, evidence, data/integration exposure, findings and owner. AI drafts findings with citations/confidence; reviewed rubric scoring and authorized human acceptance determine status. Risk acceptance carries scope, expiration, mitigation plan and owner. Expired evidence or material vendor changes create reassessment/suspension tasks. Suspended supplier blocks new request approval/PO issuance; existing obligations are flagged for a reviewed action, not automatically canceled. External continuous risk feeds require license, provenance and customer consent; no broad scraped supplier verdict is assumed.

## 9. Analytics, SaaS economics and scope — all versions

Business analytics are scoped to resource visibility/business unit and explain source currency, conversion, watermark and excluded records. Cycle-time denominators distinguish canceled/reopened/still-pending cases; spend distinguishes forecast, commitments and accounting actuals. “Savings” require a baseline/provenance and remain estimated until verified realized; no inference from AI cache avoidance to procurement savings.

Subscription billing, notification/support and organization close follow SAAS-FOUNDATION.md. Pending workflow completion and record export remain permitted under restriction where safe; AI/new-cost features may be blocked. Procurement budgets do not pay SaaS invoices. Dedicated tenant/regional tiers must run their own restore/isolation/search/capacity profile. Additional features are subject to backward-compatible migration, current security policy and measured workload requalification.
