# K2.4 supplier approval and deadline arbitration evidence

Tracked by [Keel issue #20](https://github.com/sanskarpan/Keel/issues/20) and its implementation PR (closes #20). Each changed file is committed separately.

## Delivered behavior

- Migrations `0011_supplier_case_approval_arbitration` and `0012_supplier_case_expiry_deadline_guard` add forced-RLS frozen approval plans, reviewer assignments, one-level delegations, append-only decisions and an independent no-early-expiry trigger. A deferred invariant requires the complete plan for the submitted policy/evidence snapshot.
- A human decision route accepts only a decision ID, step key, outcome and bounded reason. Tenant and actor come from trusted request context; required role comes from the frozen plan. The app role cannot write reviewer/delegation grants or update/delete decisions.
- PostgreSQL locks the case before capturing `accepted_at`, then checks the strict persisted deadline, creator/submitter separation, direct active reviewer assignment or one active non-chained delegation, and approved dependencies. It rejects terminal, duplicate-step, conflicting-ID, wrong-role, cross-tenant and stale cases.
- The decision row, immutable case event, case snapshot and minimal workflow intent commit in one transaction. Event and decision must agree on identity, actor, accepted timestamp, outcome and policy digest. Triggered Temporal dispatch remains at least once.
- `Expire` appends an immutable terminal event and intent only at/after the deadline under the same case lock, whether the case is still collecting evidence or submitted; collecting cases expire without an approval plan. It is a no-op after any already-terminal case. A separate database trigger rejects forged early expiry timestamps in both live states. Automatic Temporal timer/activity mounting is not implemented.
- The deterministic Temporal event envelope is extended from the original K2.3 maximum of 102 to 135 bounded events. The payload retains only case/event identity and hash. The transition adds no workflow commands to existing histories; K2.6 still owns full rollout/version/continuation qualification.

## Limits, privacy and rollback

The endpoint remains unmounted; there is no production identity provider, reviewer/delegation provisioning, Temporal expiry activity, paging, named on-call or customer traffic. Reviewer reasons are bounded and stored in the tenant-RLS decision table; they are excluded from Temporal payloads and telemetry. DB application credentials remain trusted workload credentials, not protection against arbitrary SQL from a stolen app login.

Migrations `0011` and `0012` are additive and have no destructive down migration. Retain decision, event, intent and Temporal history. K2.3 workers cannot process K2.4 approval event types or versions above 102, so pause dispatch during rollback and resume only with a K2.4-compatible worker after replay qualification. Do not repair/reset decisions by ad hoc SQL.
