# K8.11/K8.2 slice 10: email invites and immediate owner transfer

Related to #70 and the K8.2 track. Owners and admins grow the org by email
before the invitee has an account; acceptance seats the matching principal
and binds an unbound tenant. Owner transfer is immediate per owner
direction: the current owner becomes admin and the target becomes owner in
one audited transaction, so no path orphans an organization.
Self-transfer is a no-op success.

## Contract

- `internal/auth/organization.go`: invite windows (1 h–7 d) with
  `ValidateInviteTTL`; tokens use the new `identity-organization-invite-v1`
  purpose (registered in `TokenDigest`, covered by the separation test).
- Migration `0074_identity_invites_transfer`: forced-RLS
  `identity_invites` (unique invite id, org FK, peppered email digest,
  unique token digest, admin/member only, inviter FK, window/shape),
  pending-to-consumed guard; `invite_organization_member`
  (`not_authorized` for non-owner/admin, `already_member` when the email
  already sits in the org, global `token_reuse`, resend replacing the
  pending row); `accept_organization_invite` (atomic consume, then
  `expired`, `not_active`, `email_mismatch`, `already_member`,
  else seat with role plus bind-if-null → `accepted`);
  `transfer_organization_ownership` (ordered locking against concurrent
  transfers, actor must be owner, target must be a member, owner↔admin
  swap in one transaction, self-transfer no-op); the memberships guard
  is replaced to allow exactly the owner/admin/member↔owner transfer
  shapes. Migration `0075` forward-repairs that guard, which had refused
  the legitimate member-to-owner shape (caught live pre-commit).
- `internal/auth/postgres`: `InviteMember` (`token_reuse`→`ErrTokenReuse`),
  `AcceptInvite` (transparent outcomes), `TransferOwnership`
  (`not_authorized`→`ErrNotAuthorized`, `not_member`→`ErrNotFound`).

## Limits

- Invites carry no message, role downgrade path, or revocation beyond
  resend replacement; no expiry reminders.
- No HTTP surface for invite/accept/transfer yet; no email transport —
  tokens are returned to the caller, never mailed.
- One owner at a time only through this surface (a second owner row
  can only pre-exist outside it); no demotion/removal, no audit trail.
