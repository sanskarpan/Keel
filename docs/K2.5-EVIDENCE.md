# K2.5 implementation evidence

## Scope delivered

K2.5 adds pre-deadline cancellation for collecting and submitted supplier cases, evidence-scoped manual-review requests and resolutions, durable midpoint/final-hour reminder and deadline-expiry effects, an idempotent provider-neutral dispatcher boundary, and a 138-event Temporal envelope. Cancellation, decisions and expiry serialize on the case row. Cancellation commits its immutable event/snapshot/workflow intent and invitation revocation together; intake guards reject subsequent writes. Manual review is tied to an extracted evidence digest and cannot change the case approval state or bypass scanning/extraction controls.

Migration `0013_supplier_case_lifecycle_effects` creates the forced-RLS review and activity ledgers, enforces event/plan/role/deadline bindings, schedules stable effects, fences worker claims with lease epochs, makes effect identity/payload immutable, cancels work on terminal transitions, and exposes only lease-bound state and currently authorized principal refs to the worker. Retries persist bounded coarse error codes and retry times. Delivery remains at least once across provider acknowledgement uncertainty; an external sink must deduplicate by effect key and reject digest changes.

## Explicit operational limits

The case API and activity dispatcher are not mounted in a customer runtime. No email, chat, or other notification provider is configured or qualified; Temporal timer/activity registration, polling ownership, paging, secrets, and production SLOs remain runtime gates. Current reminder routing is limited to active principal refs for reviewers in a frozen plan. An effect that becomes due before case submission has no eligible reviewer destination and is completed as a no-op. Supplier/requester destination modeling and current-authorization rechecks are follow-up product work. These limits prevent this implementation from claiming customer-facing or hosted production readiness.

Workflow-version rollout/replay qualification and 72-hour restart evidence remain K2.6. K0/K0.1 identity, hosted dependency and accountable on-call gates remain prerequisites for enabling customer traffic.
