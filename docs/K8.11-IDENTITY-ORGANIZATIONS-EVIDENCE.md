# K8.11/K8.1 slice 7: organizations, memberships and tenant binding

Related to #70 and the K8.1 onboarding track. Principals bootstrapped
unbound (`tenant_id` NULL); this slice gives them exactly one home:
creating an organization seats the founder as owner and binds the tenant,
and owners/admins seat members whose tenants converge. Invites, owner
transfer, service principals and tenant-context wiring stay open.

## Contract

- `internal/auth/organization.go`: DNS-safe slugs (3–63, globally unique),
  bounded display names (1–128, single line), bounded region labels,
  and the fixed `owner`/`admin`/`member` roles. Owner transfer/demotion
  do not exist in this slice, so no path can orphan an organization.
- Migration `0072_identity_organizations_memberships`: forced-RLS
  `identity_organizations` and `identity_memberships` (one membership
  per principal — single-tenancy v1) with insert-only guard triggers
  (no update/delete path), and three `keel_identity`-gated SECURITY
  DEFINER functions: `create_identity_organization` (active unbound
  founder only; `not_active`/`already_bound`/`slug_conflict` with the
  slug violation distinguished by constraint name),
  `add_organization_member` (actor must hold owner/admin —
  `not_authorized` — member must be active, `already_member` and
  cross-org `tenant_conflict` refused, owner seats never granted here),
  `organizations_for` (org list with roles). The principals guard is
  replaced to permit the single NULL-to-org tenant binding while still
  refusing every other direct mutation and rebinding.
- `internal/auth/postgres`: `CreateOrganization` (`already_bound` and
  `slug_conflict` to `ErrConflict`, `not_active` to `ErrNotVerified`),
  `AddOrganizationMember` (`not_authorized` to new `ErrNotAuthorized`,
  `tenant_conflict`/`already_member` to `ErrConflict`), and
  `OrganizationsFor`.

## Verified

- `go vet ./internal/auth/...` and `gofmt` clean;
  `go test ./internal/auth/...` pass (slug/name/region/role bounds).
- Migration applied live through the runner (`0072` ledgered).
- In-container against real capability roles:
  `--- PASS: TestPostgreSQLIdentityOrganizations` — create binds the
  owner tenant and lists owner membership; second foundation and slug
  reuse are `ErrConflict`; pending founders are `ErrNotVerified`;
  owner seats member (tenant converges), member seating is
  `ErrNotAuthorized`, admin seating works, re-adding and cross-org
  adds are `ErrConflict`, owner grants and bad slugs fail before the
  database, FORCE RLS on with no grants to any non-owner across all
  ten tables, and `keel_local_app` / `keel_local_identity` denials.
- All five earlier suites pass in the same run, proving the replaced
  principals guard still gates verification, rotation, lockout and
  WebAuthn paths.

## Limits

- One membership per principal: multi-org users need an explicit later
  decision (membership cardinality, primary tenant, switching).
- No invites, no owner transfer/demotion/removal, no service
  principals, no HTTP surface, no tenant-context wiring (`tenant_id`
  is authoritative storage but no session resolver reads it yet),
  no region provisioning, no audit trail.
